# Introduction

Writing a book has been a life-long dream of mine, but I am an overthinker. I want to make it perfect and accessible to a large audience, a task that is both daunting and time consuming.

In an attempt to have a larger bias to action and recognize that "done" is better than "perfect", I am taking a slight detour. Instead of writing (and never finishing) the book I always wanted to write, I will write a book that is imperfect and personal.

If you don't know me personally, you may find this book very boring. That is okay. I hope you return and find my next book, "the book", much much nicer.

However, if you know me, you are in for a ride. Besides the excitement of this being the first book I ever wrote, the stories inside these pages follow my life throughout 2025, more precisely from September 2024 to October 2025. Why October? Because I needed 2 months to write the stories and get them printed and ready to be Christmas gifts.

2025 was a very exciting year. I moved to the USA with my wife, got my second promotion at Apple, participated in my first-ever 24 hour karting race, left my job at Apple and joined OpenAI to work on Machine Learning Infrastructure and Search products.

This book has five parts. In the first part, I will talk about how I felt 2025. These are entries from my journal, made public to the world for the first time ever. I did my best to fill in all the gaps, to make them read better for the book, but the motto of this book remains "done is better than perfect" and the chapters continue to be discrete journal entries that do not necessarily flow from one to the next.

In the second part, I will go deep into my decision to leave Apple and join OpenAI, the most exciting part of 2025 for me.

In third part, I will discuss the current state of the art in Artificial Intelligence and how I see the future of the world, given this new technology.

The fourt part is about how I saw 2025. These are entries from my blog, most of which never got published and cover topics ranging from immigration to better sleep and career advice. The chapters are totally unrelated to one another, but they all have something in common: they share my opinion about the state of the world this year.

In the fifth part, I will go back to journaling. It is very similar to the first part, except all of those stories happened much longer ago.

I hope that you enjoy and welcome aboard!
