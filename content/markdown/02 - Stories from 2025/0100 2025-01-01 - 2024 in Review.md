# 2024 Recap

It's been a pretty long 2024. Inspired by MKBHDs' https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LCpm7cNQ5Vo, this is my "What happened in 2024" entry.

## January 2024

The year started with a blur. I just returned back to Alba Iulia (Laura has been there for a bit). We did not yet have a place to stay. Laura did not want to stay in M5 anymore, so I spent the Christmas there alone. The apartment in Cluj was fine, but it was very small and working from there would have been a challenge. We found a last minute activity with Cosmin and Alexandra and we spent the NYE together at their place. First thing in 2024 was asking Alexandra to drive us home because no Uber would pick us up from Borhanci. Thanks Ale!

We spent a few more days in Cluj and headed off to Paris and Cap Verde on January 4th, where, two days later, on January 6th, I proposed (and she said YES). Laura almost died eating Caviar (actually shrimp) for the very first time and we headed off to Romania to try to find a new rental apartment to live in while also organizing our wedding, ping-pong-ing between Cluj and Alba Iulia.

We settled on both the wedding venue and the apartment before the end of January.

## February 2024

We started settling in in Cluj. I wanted to add a bit more social activity in my life, so I started the "beer with the guys" Sundays, where I'd reach out to some of my old friends and ask them whether they'd like to grab a beer (or five). Laura also wanted a bit more stability in her life, so she started going to sleep earlier and waking up earlier.

We celebrated Valentine's in the gym and Dragobete at an Irina Rimes concert (the first in a series of Irina Rimes concerts for me). The biggest attention grab for me was figuring out how to dress for my wedding and deciding who would be the "witness" from my side for the Wedding signatures, oscillating between Alex Solot and Ovidiu Olea.

Laura started a new job at Tractable and things started to be a bit more stable than December and January.

## March 2024

March started intensely with March 1st being a dinner with family, where our families met each other for the second time ever. Following that was our wedding on March 2nd. I wrote a lot about the wedding in my other articles, so I'll skip that part here. Our photographer, Daniel Nita also did an absolutely amazing job at capturing the day and I thank him.

The Formula 1 season just started and, with the apartment secured earlier in January, we had a bit more stability while figuring out all the paperwork. Laura changed her name to Cioara, so she needed a new ID, Passport, Driver License, Visa, etc. We applied for her H-4 USA visa and celebrated with a day at Therme.

Around this time, I restarted my old hobby of Kart Racing, which will become a recurring theme and recurring hobby throughout the year.

I finished the month with the second Irina Rimes concert in Cluj, where I convinced her to record a custom video for Laura.

## April 2024

We needed to be physically present in the US to apply for Laura's work authorization, so we headed there for two weeks, where I lost my beloved The North Face fleece and had to buy a replacement, expensive, Patagonia fleece which I fell in love with instantly. While here, we played golf and visited an aquarium.

We returned to Cluj just on time for Catalin Stan's wedding. Before this wedding I've only seen him once (or maybe twice) for a beer. After this wedding I haven't seen him anymore.

Around this time, I upgraded my karting helmet by adding a Pinlock anti-fog insert and by purchasing a camera to analyze my laps. This would kick start a need to actually use the new equipment.

## May 2024

For a reason that I can no longer remember, we decided to go to French Riviera for two weeks. We may have had a few extra days of holiday or something. The accommodation was awful, but the city was decent, despite the rain. We saw Monaco as well, and took some nice pictures, then we returned to Cluj for a nice Brazilian dance and dinner all-you-can-eat buffet.

End of may marked Memorial Day, which was not a holiday for Laura (who also spent all her holiday coming to Cupertino for the work permit). I would have liked to go somewhere and celebrate, but given the situation, I decided to do three days of karting at various circuits around Cluj Napoca. This kick started my desire to get better at karting.

I ate a lot of cherries, including some of the best I've ever tried at Laura's cousin and started slowly feeling the summer coming.

## June 2024

I Started off June with the regular softie ice cream, then enjoyed Irina Rimes for her third concert, for free, at the Alba Iulia city days. The weather has gotten so much better, so we started enjoying the hammock in Cluj Napoca more and we had a date on the pedal boat in Cluj central park's lake.

I trained a lot for karting and, with the help of Ralph, my new paid teacher, I managed to win my first karting competition in Cluj.

The heat has gotten so bad in Cluj, that I had to purchase a new portable AC unit, which, of course, made the room a bit more usable.

Laura had to go to London, but I went to Brasov instead and spent some time with Alex Solot for once. We did a hike that was so long that my watch died and did not record the route at all. I reunited with Laura in Munich, where we've seen Neuschwanstein Castle and her cousin.

We then finished June off with a road trip in the Balkans.

## July 2024

We spent the first two weeks of July in our yearly holiday, this time, organized by Laura, across the Balkans. We've seen so many things and took so many pictures on such a low budget, it was incredible. My only regret was that we have not seen Kosovo. My bigget highlight was seeing alpacas in Bratislava and purchasing my karting equipment (also from Bratislava)

We celebrated Laura's bday at Plitvice and I surprised her with a candle on a cake at a restaurant there.

I drove A LOT and I tried to practice smoother turns (with the car). Previously, I would steer in three discrete motions, but I tried to instead do it in one motion, which was hard and non intuitive, but I had a lot of practice over the two days. This immediately translated in better karting and I set my personal best at VMax Karting Cluj.

Due to the Balkan Trip, I missed out on the 24 hour Le Mans style karting race, but I signed up for the 9 hour one in September.

## August 2024

This month was chill. As an experiment, I wanted to taste every single beer that Romania sells and I proceeded in buying one bottle of every single beer at Auchan Cluj. I succeeded in tasting about 66% of the ones by the end of the year.

GCG organized a trip at Cabana Curmatura and I decided to bring my guitar along, but Laura was not feeling well and decided not to join me, last minute. The trip itself was meh, but it gave me the opportunity to try out the new VMax karting circuit at Brasov that just opened 2 days prior, where I scored best time of the day and 4th best overall time.

To make up for going to the cabin trip alone, I promised Laura we would go camping, which we did at a camping site in Cheile Turzii. We continued the month with a series of nice dates, following up with a Milky Way stargazing during the Perseids, then an Alternosfera concert in Sibiu.

Laura received a job offer from Google Zurich and put it her 30 day notice for Tractable. We figured out that we would not be living in Cluj anymore, so we notified Simona that we'd be leaving and we used that huge balcony for the first time ever by having a party with about 10 people, the only party I organized in 2024.

Because we were both about to leave Romania for an unknown period of time, we decided to also have a Romanian road trip and see the beauty of Romania. I finished off the month with my first karting competition on rain and this road trip where we saw Castelul de Lut from Sibiu, Vulcanii Noroiosi from Buzau and, finally, Therme for one last time.

## September 2024

We celebrated 6 months since our wedding day at a fancy restaurant in Bucharest and we headed into what would be our last 2 weeks together in Romania.

Roxana Ursu came to Romania and organized the baptism party for her son, which we attended, and, after one more quiz night date, Laura headed off to Zurich (called Geneva by me, as a joke).

After dropping Laura off, I did not have a lot of time to realize that I am all alone. I headed right off to Brasov for my 9-hour karting competition, which, again, was plagued by rain, then I headed back to Alba Iulia for Gabi Suciu's wedding, then back to Brasov for another karting competition and for trying out a professional kart for my very first time. From there, I headed straight off to Bucharest, where I saw yet another Irina Rimes' concert and attended Bogdan Popescu's wedding.

Somewhere in this entire whirlpool of events, Adrian Smarandoiu, my manager, told me that I've been promoted to ICT5 (Senior Staff Software Engineer).

## October 2024

October meant it was time for me to go to California as well. The hardest thing for me was clearing up the apartment and moving all of my belongings to either Cluj or Alba Iulia. I did not realize how much stuff there was around there.

Before leaving, I celebrated my promotion with Cosmin Rusu eating Wagyu steak at a place in Cluj.

October settled into a routine of work, talking to Laura and a daily routine that included cold showers. One important thing that I solved was putting the small house in Sacramento on the market and cleaning up the yard a bit (something that ended up costing me $3000).

I purchased a ReMarkable PRO for myself as a gift for sorting out the Sacramento studio. In the same time, the Sacramento House was behind rent by 6 months and I had to somehow sort that out as well.

I finished off the month with a Halloween walk in Los Gatos with Denis Vieriu.

## November 2024

Not much happened in early November, besides purchasing a new carry on luggage. The one I've been using since 2015 broke down after 9 years of usage (and a lot of usage by the previous owner). I researched this a lot and settled on a Briggs & Riley.

A major development for November was that Laura figured out a way to move from Zurich to the USA within Google and she started working towards that.

I flew back to Switzerland and reunited with Laura, but got instantly sick and could not enjoy the amazing snow that just fell.

Thanksgiving week meant a trip to India, which I somewhat liked and Laura absolutely hated, but we have some amazing pictures from Taj Mahal.

## December 2024

Having returned from India, we had another week in Zurich, but this time in a tiny apartment that Laura rented. To give ourselves more space, I moved all my things to the Apple office in Zurich and spent most of my time there.

We went to Zermatt for 5 days and I skied for the first time in two years. The ski effort was so big and so good that my Apple Health trends started flagging that I was healthier (avg walking and standing heart rate dropped significantly).

We boarded a plane to the USA and spent the first week trying to find accommodation in Cupertino. We settled on Markham Apartments in Cupertino, but when we moved in, the place was not ready for us and was pretty filthy and smelled like fresh paint. We (accidentally) hired a homeless person to help us move our stuff from San Jose storage to the new apartment and may have gotten bed bugs in the process.

Christmas and New Year Eve came and went. This was my first time I am spending either of these in California and they did not feel like Christmas or NYE. I did try to make the moment more memorable by buying a Christmas Tree, gifting presents to Laura (which unfortunately arrived two days late, missing Christmas) and buying Champagne and grapes for the NYE. We concluded that both celebrations were nice, but we'd like to spend these in Romania next year.

## Conclusion

2024 started on a crazy note and ended on a crazy note, but had a lot of fun and stability in the middle. The plan for 2025 is to take things down a notch, but I'd still want more excitement in my life. We'll see how that pans out.
