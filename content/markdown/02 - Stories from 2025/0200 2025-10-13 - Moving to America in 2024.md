# Moving to America in 2024

#stories
`September 1, 2024` - `December 15, 2024`

This story starts in September 2024.

Three years ago, right after meeting Laura, we had a deep conversation where we decided that we would want to both move to the US. Technically, I was already in the USA, but with the Pandemic still going strong and no ability to go to the office, I decided to spend some time in Romania, met Laura and never left.

The options were pretty straightforward:
1. Take Laura and move to the USA
2. Leave Apple and move back to Europe
3. Leave Laura and move to the USA

Since it was pretty obvious early on that leaving Laura was not an option and with Laura agreeing that USA would be a good place for her, we decided to try to get her there too.

For the following two years, we tried various avenues to get her a work visa, but they all unfortunately failed. A new hope finally came in April 2024, when we could apply for her work permit, but the processing time was 5-8 months, which meant that September 2024 was the earliest she could have started looking for a job.

We decided to spend this time in Cluj, while waiting for that permit. For some reason, despite knowing that we would not hear anything about that application until September, by August we lost all hope. Around this time, Laura received an offer to work for Google in Switzerland, something she dreamt of ever since she was a kid. We decided together that it would be a great opportunity for her to take that offer. She signed the contract on Monday. On Tuesday, she received her US work authorization.

The entire situation was bitter-sweet. On one hand, she got her dream job. On the other hand, this jeopardized our 3-year plans to move to the United States. We decided to wait and see how things evolve. We closed our rental contract in Cluj, Laura left to Zurich and I went back to California.

Just before going to California, I participated in 3 more karting competitions in Romania. One was my first-ever endurance race, in September 2024. I long wanted to participate in this race, but I missed the previous race because I was on holiday and I was missed the race before that because the weather forecast was rainy and I had no rain gear. With better equipment, I created the team and had Lucian Itu and Cosmin Pop join me for the race, a full-on rainy day that turned out to be miserable.

I also participated in a competition at VMax Karting. They called it Double Boost and had it simultaneously at all locations in the country: Cluj, Brasov, Bucharest, etc, which meant that you can only join one. I am not very good with the track in Cluj and the best people in the country are in Bucharest, so I decided to go out of my way and participate in Brasov, where I had the 4th fastest track record and I absolutely loved the circuit.

The unfortunate thing that happened was that Skillo, a racing coach from Bucharest, with an aggressive driving style, who works at VMax figured out a loophole that he could participate in the competition because, even though he worked at VMax, he worked at a different branch.

I qualified first and placed first in my heat, sending me straight to the finals. For the final qualifying, I struggled putting together a clean lap and qualified third, behind Alinush and Skillo. For the race, Skillo was supposed to have a bad kart, but, because he knew the referees, he got it changed right before the race, which really annoyed me. Upon the race start, Alinush got a bad start and, because of the nature of the track, I was able to stay together with him and close the door on Skillo on the first corner, effectively earning 2nd place and annoying Skillo in the process, because the move was very aggressive. One lap later, Alinush was still struggling and, as I opened the corner to get a better exit for the overtake, Skillo aggressively stuck his nose in the gap that I opened. For some unknown reason, I did not expect him there, I got surprised and my instinct was to close the door. Unfortunately, it was too late because he was already alongside me, so my poor decision sent me spinning 180 degrees and I felt from 2nd to 8th in one corner, effectively ruining my race. I was incredibly pissed to go from almost winning to definitely losing.

The reason I was even more pissed was that, I over extended, so that night I had to drive all the way to Alba, then all the way to Cluj to catch my flight to the US.

October and November were quiet. Laura was getting used to her new job in Switzerland and I was trying to get into a routine where I wake up at 5 AM, do some cold exposure exercises and a cold shower and work on my own projects before heading to work.

I tried cold showers in 2017 and I had to quit them because they increased my stress levels too much. I decided to give them another go this year, but again, I had to quit because my hands developed some odd symptomps where they would sweat all the time and it made it very hard for me to use a computer.

Another thing that happened is that, in recognition to my work for the past 4 years, my manager Adrian and Apple promoted me from ICT4 (Senior Software Engineer) to ICT5 (Senior Staff Software Engineer). In fact they've done that while I was still in Romania and I celebrated with Cosmin Rusu and a really nice steak. It was a good throwback to 2020, when I celebrated my promotion from ICT3 (Software Engineer) to ICT4 (Senior Software Engineer) also with Cosmin.

Mid-November, I boarded a plane straight to Zurich to see Laura for the first time in 2 months, the longest period we've been away from each other. I booked a nice AirBnB for us, but I got incredibly sick and for an entire week, I mostly only slept.

After that week, we went for a holiday in India, during the Apple company-wide closure. I found India to be incredible, and somewhat relaxing because we booked a private door-to-door tour, that picked us up from the airport and dropped us off to the airport on the last day. We've seen a bunch of things, but the Taj Mahal was the most impressive for me.

I saw the Taj Mahal in many pictures, but most of them were filled with people. I wanted to be the first one to the Taj Mahal in the morning, so that I can take a picture with absolutely no people in the background. It was my only request to our private tour and the only reason why we went private instead of group tour and I told them that I only really care about that.

The tour guide more than delivered. He told us not to bring any backpack, so that we breeze through the security and to be there really early. We got there, but we were not first in line and were pretty disappointed. The tour guide told us that the folks in front of us were newbies and brought backpacks and he was right. After breezing through the security and literally running through the gardens to see the palace, we passed the main gate to a stunning view. The Taj Mahal all for ourselves. No person but us. We were the first ones to see the palace and took picture with no one else in the background. The guide delivered, his tip was handsome and I was satisfied.

We were pretty lucky not to get food poisoning in India, something that happens often, but at the same time, we followed the rule "peel it, boil it or throw it". Additionally, we also tried to stay away from sketchy stuff and some days we'd only eat toasted bread with boiled egg, but we were fine and greatly enjoyed our time there.

Coming back to Zurich early December, we booked a Ski trip to Zermatt, so that I can continue practicing skiing. We got Sorin and Andra with us and it was one of the nicest holidays in a while, albeit a bit expensive.

Returning from Zermatt, the plan was to spend the New Year Eve in Tunisia, as we found some really cheap tickets and we never visited the country before. Laura wanted to spend Christmas in Romania, but I was somewhat opposed to the idea because I was stressed with what happens after January: would we continue to live in two separate continents or would something change.

Something did change. In a flash of inspiration, Laura noticed that her team was recruiting in Mountain View. She asked the hiring manager if they needed an internal transfer from Zurich (her). Normally, Laura would not be allowed to change teams within her first year and not allowed to change countries within her first two years. However, since it was the same team, the transfer was not considered "transfer" and it got approved. The catch was that we had to be in the USA as soon as possible, before January 1st.

We packed our stuff, cancelled the rent in Zurich, booked an AirBnB in USA, cancelled our New Year Eve trip to Tunisia, abandoned our plans to go to Romania, boarded a plane and moved to the United States for real, together, with the right for the both of us to work in the United States.
