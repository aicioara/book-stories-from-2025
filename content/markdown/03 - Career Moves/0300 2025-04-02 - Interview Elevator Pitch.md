# Interview Elevator Pitch

`April 2, 2025`

Hello. My name is Andrei and I am a Software Engineer with 12 years of industry experience. I started my career in tech, working for the likes of Palantir and Microsoft, then ventured a bit into High Frequency Trading with Jump Trading and Citadel Securities, then I co-founded a start-up, got to speak with YCombinator, who did not invest in us, for good reason. After that, I was pretty successful doing freelancing for a while. Eventually, I joined Apple, where I've been for the past 6 years.

My career at Apple was very successful. I joined as a Software Engineer, got promoted to Senior Engineer pretty soon and then again to Senior Staff Software Engineer. During these 6 years, I launched to production many user-facing features, including Web Images, Visual Look Up, UI overhauls for Siri, Report an Issue pipelines and many others that have not launched yet.

My most recent and the biggest project was Enhanced Visual Look Up, where I was the DRI for bringing the project to production. This was a 2.5 year long project where my role was to design the architecture of the entire system, implement the key back-end services and lead a virtual team of 10-20 engineers to deliver the feature. More detailed, I had to:
- Design the back-end architecture, client-server APIs and the dev-ops processes
- Lead the rollout predictions, cost estimations and cost approvals
- Act as the first point of contact for the client engineering team
- Act as the first point of contact for the ranking and quality team
- Work with cross-functional team to enable OHTTP gateways and proper network traffic distribution for our service
- Implement the adoption of an in-house, experimental, homomorphic encryption library
- Personally deliver on all the checkboxes for a new production system: integration with the build system, privacy approval, on-call runbooks, etc
- Implement key features of the back-end system: indexing pipeline, online serving infrastructure

I've done all of these while simultaneously delivering on 3 other concurrent projects.
