# Introduction

One of the most important things that happened in 2025 was that I changed jobs from Apple to OpenAI. This was a really big change that also brought a lifestyle shift.

In this part, I will go deep into the story of how I changed the jobs, the events that preceded this move and those that came after. I will reproduce some social media announcements and some emails that I sent to my teammates as I was departing one company and joining the other. But first, I must set the stage. 

In 2025, OpenAI was the hottest company in the Bay Area and possibly in the world. It was a dream of mine to work here ever since November 2022 when I saw ChatGPT, their core product, for the first time. However, my life circumstances made it hard for me to take a shot at it. In particular, my top priority was always to move to the USA with Laura, so a job change to either OpenAI or anyone else was never on the table.

Before changing jobs, I would have worked at Apple for a bit over 6 years. When I joined Apple in 2019, I was at a very high point in my career. I just started an outsourcing company in Romania a few months prior. The industry was booming and my small business took off, only for me to hit pause when the offer from Apple and the USA visa came knocking on my door. Apple low-balled me with both the title and the salary, so I mainly accepted the offer because it was my only opportunity to activate the visa and to leave the door open for a potential later return.

I arrived with the intention to only activate my visa and quit, so I also purchased the return ticket, 3 months later. However, during those three months, I fell in love with California and with Apple. The job was very satisfying and relaxing, my manager was amazing and I managed to have impact with my work on billions of people worldwide. More importantly, I felt a sense of guilt towards leaving right away because my director went through a lot of trouble to secure a USA visa for me, something that I am still deeply grateful for.

After those three months, my new goal became leaving after 1 year, but in 2020, a global pandemic made the entire world very unpredictable. I decided that it was better to stay where I was and watch what is going on, instead of making waves with a career change.

In 2022, both my manager and my director left Apple, so I was also officially freed from the moral obligation to stay. I started looking around for a new job, but I quickly realized that any change would affect my lifestyle. At that time, my top priority was figuring out a way for Laura to move to the USA with me. A job change would have made me unavailable for the ramp-up months, and would have taken away some of the freedoms that Apple was giving me, so I decided that it was wiser to just wait until we are officially settled in the USA.

In December 2024 we finally settled in the USA, so 3 months later, in the middle of March, I responded to one of OpenAI's cold emails and started the interview process.

Despite all of that, leaving Apple after 6 years was in fact a very hard decision. I really liked it there, I really felt like home, and I will always remember these years very dearly.
