# Apple Introduction Email

`April 3, 2025`

Hello everyone! My name is Andrei and I am excited to transfer from the Siri & Information Intelligence (SII) org and join the Pegasus Ads & Recommendations Team in MLPT.

My tenure at Apple started 6 years ago, when I briefly joined the Web Answers team, before transferring to a brand-new team that was just getting started, called Argos. For the following five and a half years, the Argos team and I launched Web Images ("Hey Siri, show me images for corgi from the web"), Visual Look Up, SMART UI modernization of Web Images in Siri, Enhanced Visual Look Up using Private Information Retrieval (PII) via Homomorphic Encryption, the initial deployment of the CLIP model to production, and many, many other amazing projects.

My primary contribution over the years has been taking projects from idea to production, focusing on the server infrastructure and stakeholder alignment. I fortunately already know many of you, as we worked together on the Pegasus APIs, cost approvals, performance tuning, various tooling for Go, Rust or Swift, Pegasus Tech Talks, or the occasional PBR. If we haven't met yet, let's grab a coffee ASAP, and if we did, do not hesitate to say hi when you see me in KF04.

Before Apple, I worked in tech (Microsoft and Palantir), High Frequency Trading (Jump Trading and Citadel Securities), I co-founded (and subsequently failed) a startup, and eventually established a brand for my contracting agency, just before joining Apple.

In my free time, I pursue an array of interests. I love competitive kart racing, video editing and I combine the two in a small YouTube channel. I enjoy writing and I used to have a blog with 100K+ readers. I am obsessed with `#shotoniphone` photography and a big goal of mine is to be featured in one of the internal Apple competitions that happen annually. I love conducting experiments, and my latest one is improving the gas efficiency out of my Toyota Prius, my target being an average of 70 MPG over 12 months. You will also see me playing my guitar, dancing bachata, attending competitive programming competitions or, when vacation allows, traveling the world with my wife.

I am looking forward to working even closer together and to getting to know many of you.
