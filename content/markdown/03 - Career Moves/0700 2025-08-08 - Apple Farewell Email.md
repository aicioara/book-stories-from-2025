Dear friends and colleagues,

After careful consideration and lots of deliberation, I made the difficult decision to leave Apple after 6 amazing years spent here. My last day is today, Monday, July 28.

These years have been the highlight of my career, and I am grateful for the opportunity to work and learn from each of you. I spent the week-end replaying in my head all the highlights from Apple and, while I am excited for what is next, I am incredibly sad that I am closing the favorite chapter of my career by far.

I still remember the excitement when joining the newly formed Argos team, the uncertainty when the pandemic forced us to work remotely, the celebration when I pressed the launch button for Siri Web Images, the pride I felt when I saw Craig introduce Visual Look Up at WWDC'21, the dedication when I interrupted my vacation to snipe PR #100,000 in luna/beam, and the joy when we launched Enhanced Visual Search to 80,000 QPS (and nothing crashed).

With this final email, I want to thank the almost one hundred colleagues who worked with me over the years, who shaped me into a better engineer and, more importantly, into a better person. Thank you for your stories and thank you for your patience.

I want to thank Alper Kokmen, Erich Schmidt and Fabian Boemer for their quick turnaround in reviewing over 1,000 of my pull requests and for all of their feedback. You could have taken days, but instead you reviewed in minutes and you had the biggest impact on my productivity, as well as the engineering excellence of the entire org.

I also want to thank my current and former managers, each for the unique career guidance and life lessons that you shared with me over the years: Steven Guo, Michael Emmersberger, Adrian Smarandoiu, Ievgen Soboliev and Helen Song.

I also thank Yang Zhao, Frank Chu and Michael Xu for all the opportunities that you offered me over the years and for being super generous with your time.

Finally I want to thank my informal mentor, Uday Rayala, for all of your guidance throughout the years, from my first months at Apple, when you showed me how to setup the GoLand IDE and all the way to last week when you showed me how to setup cargoscan to identify vulnerabilities in docker images. You have been a continuous inspiration to me.

I will still live in Cupertino for the foreseeable future and I hope that we stay in touch and grab the occasional coffee. Let me know if I can help with anything.
