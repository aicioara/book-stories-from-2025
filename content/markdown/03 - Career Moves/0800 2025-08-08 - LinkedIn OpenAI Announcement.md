I am happy to finally share that I am joining OpenAI as a Member of Technical Staff (Principal Software Engineer), focusing on Machine Learning Infrastructure for ChatGPT.

Working at OpenAI has been a dream of mine ever since ChatGPT passed my personal Turing Test in 2022 and made me realize that we may actually achieve AGI in my lifetime. The pace of innovation in the Machine Learning space is something I haven't experienced since the early internet days and I feel incredibly lucky that my career intersects this era with the opportunity to take a front-row seat.

Landing this opportunity has been a team effort and I am incredibly grateful to the people in my support system, particularly to Laura Cioara, who not only gave me the space and time to prepare for the interviews every day for 7 months, but also watched all the System Design videos with me to help me stay awake and focused.

I want to once again share a massive thanks to my mentor Dima Soboliev for nearly a decade of career guidance and for being my biggest influence since 2017. Thank you for all the coaching and for rooting for me.

I also thank my three mock interviewers who generously helped me brush up on my skills without judging my early missteps. Thanks to Cosmin Carabet who set aside part of his birthday for the mock, to Vlad Ionescu who interrupted his vacation to help, and to Sorin Mircea who tricked me into designing his side project as a mock. Now, back to work. More updates to come as we launch new things.
