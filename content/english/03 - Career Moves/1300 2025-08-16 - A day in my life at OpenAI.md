# A day in my life at OpenAI

I am writing this mainly to remember my work schedule for the first 3 weeks at OpenAI. I am sure things will change.

- I go into the office 5 days a week
- While I am in the office, I rarely take breaks or use the phone
- I burnt out once, on August 14. I took 1 hour in the library to recover. It was probably related to food digestion or a glycemic drop
- I only eat breakfast (at 8 AM) and dinner (at 6 PM)

This is how an average day goes
- 05:30 - With my latest sleep trick (sleep with the window open), I naturally wake up pretty early
- 06:00 - Alarm. Get ready, chit chat with Laura
- 07:00 - Go to work. Plus or Minus 15 minutes
- 07:50 - Arrive at work and check emails
- 08:00 - Have breakfast
- 08:30 - Finish breakfast and go straight to work
- 18:00 - Go to dinner
- 18:30 - Finish dinner
- 18:40 - Now it depends, sometimes I leave right after dinner (18:40), other times I go back to work and work until 19:30. And if I take the Caltrain, I must leave at 18:00. I'd say most days, I leave at 18:40
- 19:30 - Arrive home. If I stayed longer, then 20:30. I do not open the laptop. I spend time with Laura. Generally, we have a few options: go for a walk, go for a swim, stay for a beer and chatter at home
- 21:00 - Attempt to go to to my room. Usually I only manage at 21:30
- 21:30 - Attempt to sleep. Usually I only manage to sleep at 22:00
