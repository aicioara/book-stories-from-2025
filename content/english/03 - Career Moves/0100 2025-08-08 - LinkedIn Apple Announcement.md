After careful consideration and lots of deliberation, I made the difficult decision to leave Apple after 6 amazing years spent at the company.

These years have been the highlight of my career, and I am grateful for the opportunity to work with and learn from many exceptionally smart colleagues. While I am excited about the future, I am also incredibly sad about closing this chapter. Today, I want to take a minute to reflect on my time there.

I vividly remember the excitement I felt when joining the newly formed Visual Intelligence team, the uncertainty when the pandemic forced us to work remotely, the deep satisfaction of pressing the launch button for Siri Web Images, the pride when I saw Craig Federighi introduce Visual Look Up at WWDC'21, and the joy when we launched Enhanced Visual Look Up, my biggest project (and nothing crashed).

With this final post, I want to thank the almost one hundred colleagues who worked with me over the years, who shaped me into a better engineer and, more importantly, a better person. Thank you for your stories and thank you for your patience. For privacy and practicality, I will not enumerate all names, as I did in my internal email, but you know who you are: my managers, mentors, enablers, leadership and colleagues.

I thank Apple for everything they offered me over the years and for the opportunity to work on amazing products. I cannot wait to see next year's launches! Please continue collaborating, innovating, and building these amazing products that I, my family, and the entire world deeply love.

I will share more details about what is next on Monday. Have a great weekend!

Andrei
