# Conclusion

If you read everything, from the beginning until now, thank you!

Thank you for joining me on this journey and I hope that you enjoyed the time that we spent together. I certainly enjoyed writing for you.

When I set out to write a book, I knew that it was not going to be easy. However, I did not know just how hard it was going to be. In fact, if I knew it was going to be that hard, I would probably not have started in the first place (so I am glad that I did not know).

The most difficult part of the entire process were the sacrifices that had to be made. I started writing this book after I joined OpenAI and I had to finish it before going on my holiday to China in mid November, so that it has sufficient time to be ready by Christmas 2025.

With 14+ hour week-days at OpenAI, the only time I had to write on the book were the week-ends. However, week-ends are also the times to enjoy nature, go on holidays, spend time with Laura, socialize, clean the house, fix things that break, run errands, sleep and rest.

I decided to prioritize OpenAI, my time with Laura, the hiking challenge and the book, so everything else was sent to sacrifice. The house became increasingly dirtier, broken things would just stay broken, emails would pile in my inbox, texts from friends would go unanswered and piles of paper would mount up on my desk, waiting to be addressed. One fun fact is that the new iPhone 17 Pro that I opened sit on my desk, in its box, for a week, before I finally took the time to check it out.

All of my sleep hacks (if you read that chapter) got me going strong for three months, but around November, I got super tired, so everything became much harder: from the job to the writing to my relationship. But I have no regrets, other than the fact that writing the final chapters of this book on accumulated fatigue suffered in terms of quality. I hope you still liked them though.

While I do not promise a similar book next year, I already have a couple of ideas for how to improve the process, so that I can also have a life alongside writing. I hope to see you next year, but in the mean time, you can subscribe to my newsletter at https://family.andreicioara.com.

With that said, I will leave you with a couple of closing thoughts.
